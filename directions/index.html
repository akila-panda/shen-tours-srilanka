Shen Tours Sri Lanka: three design directions

Each one is built around the road: Shen sells a driver-guide, a car and the drive across the island. Pick one (or parts of several) and the homepage gets rebuilt in that direction.

  • The road book

    A. The Road Book (recommended)

    Shen's route notebook. Laterite road-red, hand-inked route with his notes, railway-ticket tour cards, Sinhala and Tamil stop names.

  • Window seat

    B. Window Seat

    A day across the island seen from the van. Photos in window frames, and the page colour moves from dawn to night as you scroll.

  • Painted roads

    C. Painted Roads

    Sri Lankan lorry and bus art. Drenched marigold, sign-painter letters, floral corners and trilingual destination boards.

Internal review pages. Photos are free Unsplash images of Sri Lanka, loaded from Unsplash and not yet downloaded; they get replaced by approved or owner photos. Handwriting, Sinhala and Tamil spellings need Shen's check. Prices and facts come from the same data files as the homepage.
